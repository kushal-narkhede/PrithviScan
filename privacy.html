Trust & ownership

Your data, your farm

Farmers own their field data. Sharing is always optional.

You own your data

Fields, notes, photos, yields, and feedback you enter belong to you. You can export or delete them.

Optional aggregated insights

If you opt in, we may use anonymized, aggregated signals (never your name or exact plot identity in public datasets) to improve regional models. You can turn this off anytime in Profile.

Revenue sharing (planned)

When aggregated insight products generate revenue, opted-in contributors may receive credits or revenue share. Terms will be published before any program launches.

Safety & disclaimers

PrithviScan provides decision support, not a substitute for local agronomist advice. High-risk actions (e.g. pesticide application) require your confirmation and may be flagged for human review.
